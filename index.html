Selected Projects

Case studies in content and search.

SEO Content Writing · Content Blogging

How to Care for Hiking Boots: Cleaning, Conditioning and Maintenance Guide

Personal portfolio piece — outdoor & footwear niche

Challenge
Outdoor gear care is a crowded search topic where most articles are thin, repetitive and written for the search engine rather than the reader.
Strategy
Build the piece around a clear search intent — owners looking for practical maintenance steps — and structure it as a genuinely useful long-form guide with scannable sections.
Execution
Researched the topic and related search terms, outlined the article around cleaning, conditioning and maintenance, and wrote it in a clear, instructional voice with a logical heading hierarchy and supporting imagery.
Expected Outcomes
A search-ready, reader-first guide demonstrating long-form SEO writing: keyword-informed structure, readable formatting and content designed to stay useful over time.
