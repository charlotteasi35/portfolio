Selected Projects

Case studies in content and search.

Graphic Design · Social Media Marketing

Chasi Skincare Product Range Flyer

Chasi Skincare — beauty & personal care

Situation
Chasi Skincare needed a single promotional visual that could introduce its full product range and carry a limited-time offer across social channels.
Task
Design a flyer that presents every product clearly, communicates the offer at a glance and gives customers one obvious way to order.
Action
Designed the flyer in Canva around a warm, earthy palette drawn from the product packaging, arranged the oil, lotion, moisturizer, body cream and scrub as a single hero line-up, set a bold brand masthead, and placed the discount badge and call-to-order phone number where the eye finishes reading.
Result
A ready-to-publish promotional asset that shows the full range in one frame, keeps the brand look consistent and directs interested customers straight to a phone order.

SEO Optimization · Analytics

SEO Research & Visibility Tracking with Moz

Self-directed practice — SEO & analytics

Situation
Content decisions are easy to make on instinct, but without search data it is guesswork which topics are worth writing about.
Task
Build a routine for researching keywords and tracking how content performs in search, so topic choices are evidence-led.
Action
Worked in the Moz dashboard to run keyword research, review domain authority and link signals, and read performance by inbound channel — search, social, links and brand mentions — then translated those signals into a content priority list.
Result
A repeatable research-to-publish workflow where every topic is chosen against search data and reviewed afterwards, rather than published and forgotten.
MozGoogle Keyword PlannerGoogle Search ConsoleGoogle Analytics

SEO Optimization · Competitive Research

Backlink & Competitive Analysis with Majestic

Self-directed practice — SEO & analytics

Situation
Search visibility depends as much on who links to a site and what competitors rank for as it does on the content itself.
Task
Learn to read backlink profiles and competitor keyword positions, and turn that into practical recommendations for content and outreach.
Action
Used the dashboard to review backlink volume and referring domains, compare organic against paid keyword coverage, study top organic keywords and position distribution, and note where content gaps and link opportunities sat.
Result
A clear method for auditing a site's authority and competitive position, feeding into content topics and link-building priorities.
MajesticMozGoogle Search Console

Personal portfolio piece — outdoor & footwear niche

Situation
Outdoor gear care is a crowded search topic where most articles are thin, repetitive and written for the search engine rather than the reader.
Task
Produce a long-form guide that matches real search intent — owners looking for practical maintenance steps — and reads better than what already ranks.
Action
Researched the topic and related search terms, outlined the article around cleaning, conditioning and maintenance, and wrote it in a clear, instructional voice with a logical heading hierarchy and supporting imagery.

A search-ready, reader-first guide demonstrating long-form SEO writing: keyword-informed structure, readable formatting and content designed to stay useful over time.
